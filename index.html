Telco Customer Churn Predictor

A Gradio app that estimates whether a telecom customer is likely to leave. It loads a saved scikit-learn Random Forest and the label encoders from training, then returns a stay-or-leave result with a probability. The training pipeline, with feature engineering and cross-validation, reached 85%+ accuracy.
